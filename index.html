What the runtime guarantees

non-negotiables, enforced before output

01No fabrication

If extraction fails, is blocked, or hits a paywall, the run stops and asks for raw text. Unreachable sources never become plausible summaries.

02Verified metrics only

Every number in a post must appear in the source. No rounded figures, no recalled benchmarks, no invented speedups.

03No outcome promises

No guaranteed career results and no fixed learning timelines. Posts 1 to 4 carry no promotion at all.

04Terms kept exact

Protocols and algorithms keep their industry name, followed by a plain-language gloss the reader can use immediately.
